1.1.2 Vertical Line Test (VLT)¶

Mathematical Concept: A relation is a function if any vertical line intersects its graph at most once. $$ y^2 = x \implies y = \pm\sqrt{x} $$

Modeling Objective: Geometrically demonstrate a non-function relation using vertical line intersections.

↑ Back to Table of Contents

In [4]:
vline!([50], label="VLT Line at x=50", color=:red, linestyle=:dash, lw=2)
Out[4]:
In [5]:
x_var = 0:0.1:100
y_p = sqrt.(x_var)
y_n = -sqrt.(x_var)

plot(x_var, y_p, label="y = +√x" ,color=:gray)
plot!(x_var, y_n, label="y = -√x" ,color=:gray)

vline!([25], label="VLT Line at x=25", color=:red, linestyle=:dash, lw=2)
Out[5]:
